Free Spin the Wheel Online

Spin the Wheel is a fast random picker wheel for everyday choices. Add any list of options, press the spin button, and let the wheel select one answer for you. It is useful when every option is possible and you want a neutral, playful way to decide.

You can use it as a food wheel, name picker wheel, classroom spinner, chore wheel, prize wheel, party prompt wheel, or random decision wheel. The tool runs in your browser, so it is easy to use on desktop, tablet, or mobile.

FAQ

Is the wheel result random?

Yes. The page picks from your list with browser randomness and animates the wheel to that result.

Can I use it as a name wheel?

Yes. Paste one name per line and spin to pick a random person.

Can I edit the options?

Yes. You can replace the starter list with your own names, tasks, foods, or ideas.
