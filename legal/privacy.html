Privacy Policy

Play Picks is designed as a simple browser-based tools site. Custom lists you type into tools run in your browser unless a future feature clearly says otherwise.

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate page views and tool interactions, such as button clicks and game page visits. Analytics data helps us improve the site experience and decide which games to build next.

Play Picks may display advertising, including Google AdSense, after approval. Advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to measure ad performance and, where allowed, personalize ads.

You can manage cookies and advertising personalization through your browser settings and Google account ad settings. You can also contact us at gaoxiangfengcd@gmail.com.
