How to play Carpet Cleaning

Pick a tool, hold the left mouse button over the rug, and drag across the dirty areas. Soap softens dark grease, water rinses broad dirt, and the brush removes stubborn mud faster.

FAQ

Is this a real cleaning simulator?

No. It is a relaxing browser mini game focused on satisfying dirty-to-clean feedback, not real fluid physics.

Can I play without installing anything?

Yes. This MVP runs directly in the browser with HTML canvas and local game state.
